# Third-party rights and publication status

**Unofficial experimental public beta. The project is not approved by Riot Games; publication does not certify that its methods or third-party assets are permitted. Use at your own risk.**

## Original assets shipped by this project

`src/Assets/app-symbol.svg`, `avatar-*.svg`, `app.ico`, `app-icon*.png` and `avatar-*.png` are original geometric artwork for this application. They replace the game's logos and the prior Riot-like fist symbol. They are included under this project's MIT license; they are not official Riot logos.

Textual names like Riot Client, VALORANT, League of Legends and Teamfight Tactics refer to third-party products, owned by their respective rightsholders. No endorsement or affiliation is implied.

## Official Riot Data Dragon (LoL / TFT)

Riot's [League of Legends developer documentation](https://support-developer.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/22698698001939-League-of-Legends) describes Data Dragon game data and profile icons as usable by third-party developers, subject to Riot's applicable API policies and terms. The application shows these images only when users explicitly browse the online gallery; they are not included in the source or executable. Confirm policy compliance before a public release.

## Community Valorant-API player cards

[Valorant-API](https://valorant-api.com/) is an **unofficial fan service** that exposes VALORANT game assets on `media.valorant-api.com`. No user profiles or account data are retrieved from the provider. The user must click to load its catalog. Images are Riot Games intellectual property and **service availability or free access alone does not grant all distribution rights**; review applicable terms and the game's developer policies.

## OP.GG removal

The v0.7.0 source **removes all automated OP.GG scraping, HTTP requests and source parsing**. No account name or Riot ID is sent to OP.GG.

## Riot Client session switching

The project still restores locally saved sessions through undocumented Riot Client configuration files. This is **not** an official authentication integration, and no authorization for distribution of this mechanism has been established. Review [Riot's developer policies](https://developer.riotgames.com/policies/general) and [legal policy](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal), then seek clarification/approval if necessary when possible, and do not represent the application as approved or risk-free.
